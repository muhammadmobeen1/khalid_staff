Khalid Networks partial payment update
- Receive amount defaults to total customer due (current + previous pending).
- Actual received amount can be entered manually from > 0 up to total due.
- Overpayment above the customer's dynamic total due is blocked.
- Payments are allocated automatically across unpaid bills through the selected month.
- Remaining balance stays pending automatically.
- Staff provisional receipts use the same total/received/remaining calculation.
- 58mm receipt RECEIVED RS amount uses smaller text to avoid wrapping.
